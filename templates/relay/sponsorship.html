THE EVENT

The Welsh Castles Relay is one of the most iconic endurance relay races in the UK.

The 20-stage, 209-mile event runs from Caernarfon Castle in North Wales to Cardiff Castle in the south, with teams competing across an entire weekend through some of the toughest and most scenic stages in Wales.

Swansea Harriers return in 2026 with one of the strongest squads the club has fielded in years.
